# Hutchrok Full Launch — Subscription Filing Model

Launch date: 2026-08-30

## Core Positioning

Hutchrok is a veteran-focused business operating platform. Filing service is now a benefit of active Hutchrok membership rather than a standalone public loss leader. Membership tier controls the annual included filing allowance, Hutchrok internal preparation priority, subscriber discounts, and depth of advisory/service access.

Government filing fees are separate unless waived by an applicable government program. Government processing and approval timelines are controlled by the relevant agency. Hutchrok priority language refers only to Hutchrok's internal preparation queue after complete intake and required documents are received.

## Plans

- Operator — $59/month — 1 included Hutchrok filing service per membership year — standard internal preparation target 7–10 business days — 10% eligible-service discount.
- Growth — $129/month — 2 included Hutchrok filing services per membership year — priority internal preparation target 3–5 business days — 15% eligible-service discount.
- Command — $249/month — 4 included Hutchrok filing services per membership year — front-of-queue internal preparation target 1–2 business days — 20% eligible-service discount.

## Expanded Subscriber Value

Membership should be marketed as an operating system, not a filing club. Subscriber value includes:

- Entity formation and filing preparation/submission benefits by tier
- Compliance calendars, deadline reminders, and filing tracking
- Veteran verification and filing-readiness guidance
- Business websites, domains, business email, brand identity, and launch infrastructure
- Business-credit readiness, vendor stack guidance, and funding preparation
- SAM.gov, SDVOSB/VOSB, certification and federal contracting readiness
- Bid-readiness and opportunity support for higher tiers
- Automation, AI workflow, digital infrastructure and operational consulting
- Veteran Intelligence: business opportunities, funding, benefits, housing/homeownership, education, employment, family resources and high-impact VA/SBA/state updates
